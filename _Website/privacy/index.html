Support & Privacy

Support

Questions, bug reports, and requests are all welcome. Email support@playvariaball.com and I'll get back to you.

It helps to include your device model and OS version, and — if something looks wrong — a screenshot or clip.

Privacy Policy

Variaball collects nothing. That is the whole of it, but here are the specifics.

  • No data is collected. There is no Variaball server, no Variaball account, and no sign-in of our own. Nothing about you or how you play is sent to the developer.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising. The game contains no third-party SDKs of any kind, and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Your progress stays with you. Settings, customizations, and unlocks are stored on the device and are readable only by this game. Deleting the game deletes them.
  • Online play goes through Game Center. Matchmaking, invites, and friends are handled by Apple's Game Center. During an online match, the game exchanges only gameplay inputs with your opponent's device, and shows your Game Center name to them. None of it is stored by Variaball. Apple's own handling of Game Center is covered by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Purchases go through Apple. Unlocking the full game is handled entirely by the App Store. Apple — not this game — receives the payment details, and no payment information is ever visible to or stored by Variaball.
  • Children. Since no data is collected from anyone, none is collected from children.

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date below. Questions about it can go to the same address as support.
