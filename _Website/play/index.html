Play the Alpha

The Variaball public alpha is free on iPhone and iPad through TestFlight, Apple's app for testing games before release.

Join

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store.
  2. On the same device, open the invite: testflight.apple.com/join/S8VDg2cr.
  3. Tap Accept, then Install.

Feedback

Take a screenshot while playing and TestFlight offers to send it with a note. Bugs, crashes, and ideas are all welcome, or email support@playvariaball.com.
